# Customer Login Flow

## Goal

Allow the customer to identify themselves without creating a traditional account.

---

## Entry

User clicks:

Immerse

↓

Connect with Telegram

---

## Authentication

Telegram confirms the user's identity.

PREVIA receives a Telegram ID.

---

## Customer

If the customer is new:

- Create Customer.

If the customer already exists:

- Load Customer.

---

## Session

Vintage App receives Customer ID.

Customer becomes available to the application.

---

## Features

Available immediately:

- Favorites synchronization.

Future:

- Personal vintage search.
- Collector privileges.
- Purchase history.
- Reserved items.
- Loyalty program.
